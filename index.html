WHAT WE DO

Operational support built around your requirements.

We provide an external coordination and support function for maritime companies, helping organise tasks that require time, local resources or additional operational capacity.

01

Operations & Coordination

Coordination of port-related tasks, local service providers, contractors and operational requirements. We help organise resources and follow assignments through to completion.

02

Procurement & Supply

Sourcing of marine spare parts, equipment, provisions and technical supplies. We assist with supplier identification, quotation collection, offer comparison and delivery coordination.

03

Technical & Project Support

Coordination of repairs, maintenance activities, technical services and project-related tasks, including communication with local contractors and progress follow-up.

04

Compliance & Operational Readiness

Support with inspection preparation, compliance documentation, follow-up of identified deficiencies and coordination of qualified specialists where independent technical services are required.

ABOUT AC MARITIME

An independent maritime operations support company.

AC MARITIME TRANSPORT & CARGO SAFETY S.R.L. is a Romania-based company focused on outsourced maritime operations support for shipowners, ship managers and maritime businesses.

We help clients organise and coordinate operational tasks by connecting requirements with suitable service providers, suppliers and independent maritime specialists.

Our role is to provide a structured coordination function: understanding the requirement, defining the scope, organising the resources and following the assignment through to completion.

We work on an assignment basis or within an agreed ongoing support arrangement, depending on the client's operational needs.

OUR OPERATING MODEL

Independent coordination supported by specialist providers.

AC Maritime acts as an operational coordination and support partner. Specialist technical work, statutory inspections, certification and other regulated activities are performed by appropriately qualified and authorised independent providers where required.

The scope, responsibilities, provider arrangements and commercial terms are agreed with the client before each assignment begins.
